## 🏥 Problem

Today, our medical information is scattered across paper prescriptions, WhatsApp images, email attachments, hospital portals, diagnostic reports, and our own memory.

When we or our family members visit a doctor, we often struggle to answer basic but important questions:

* What happened during my previous visit?
* Why was I prescribed this medication?
* Which doctor suggested this treatment?
* What tests have I already taken?
* What were my previous symptoms and diagnoses?
* What should I tell a new doctor about my medical history?

This becomes even more difficult when managing the health of parents, spouses, or children. We don't just lose records—we lose the **context behind our healthcare journey**.

Existing health apps primarily focus on storing reports, booking appointments, or providing individual services. They don't necessarily help users maintain a simple, continuous, family-wide health history that is easy to understand and share.

## 💡 Solution

We are thinking of building **HealthVault**, a personal and family health memory platform that keeps all health-related information organized in one place.

Users can create profiles for themselves and their family members and continuously add health updates such as:

* Doctor visits and reasons for consultation
* Symptoms and health concerns
* Doctor recommendations
* Medications and why they were prescribed
* Lab tests and medical reports
* Doctor and hospital details
* Follow-up instructions
* Past medical events

The platform converts this information into a **structured, chronological health timeline**, so users can easily understand what happened over time.

For example, instead of searching through WhatsApp or old files, a user can simply open the app and see:

> “In May, I visited Dr. X for fatigue. The doctor suggested a thyroid test. In June, I started medication Y. In July, I had a follow-up.”

## 🚀 What Makes It Different

This is not just a medical document locker.

It is a **health memory system** that remembers the context behind every medical event.

The platform can also:

* Generate a doctor-ready medical history summary
* Allow users to share their history with a new doctor
* Support multiple family health profiles
* Provide reminders for follow-ups
* Adapt the experience for specific health journeys such as pregnancy, child health, or chronic condition management
* Surface safe, history-based insights and missing-information prompts

## 🎯 Core Vision

> **People shouldn't have to remember their entire healthcare journey or run behind medical files. Their health history should be organized, accessible, and available whenever they need it.**

**HealthVault: Your health history. Your family's health memory. Always with you.**
