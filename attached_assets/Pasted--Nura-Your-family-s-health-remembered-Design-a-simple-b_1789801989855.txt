# Nura — Your family's health, remembered.

Design a **simple, beautiful, modern family health management app called Nura**.

### Tagline

**“Your family's health, remembered.”**

The product should feel **calm, trustworthy, warm, simple, and easy for anyone in a family to understand**.

Do NOT make it look like a complicated medical dashboard or a hospital management system.

The primary goal is:

> **Help families remember, organize, and understand what happened to each family member's health over time.**

---

# 1. Visual Design

Use a **soft sky-blue + white visual theme**.

### Colors

* Very light sky blue
* Soft blue
* White
* Very subtle grey for secondary text
* Dark navy/blue-grey only for important text

Avoid:

* Dark backgrounds
* Purple
* Green-heavy healthcare colors
* Bright red
* Strong gradients
* Heavy shadows
* Overly colorful cards

The interface should feel **light, spacious, peaceful, and premium**.

Use:

* Rounded cards
* Soft borders
* Simple icons
* Large readable typography
* Plenty of whitespace
* Minimal navigation

---

# 2. Main Product Concept

Nura should organize family health around **people + health issues + incidents + timelines**.

There are two types of health situations:

### A. Short-term Health Issue

Example:

* Left shoulder pain
* Fever
* Cold
* Minor injury
* Temporary infection

These usually have a beginning and an expected recovery.

Nura should ask:

> **“Is this something temporary, or something you may need to manage over a longer period?”**

If temporary:

* Start date
* What happened
* Doctor visit
* Medication
* Tests/reports
* Expected recovery
* Follow-up/reminder

Then the issue can eventually be marked **Resolved**.

---

### B. Long-term Health Journey

Examples:

* Thyroid
* PCOS / PCOD
* Diabetes
* Hypertension
* Asthma
* Long-term medication
* Pregnancy
* Other ongoing health conditions

For these, Nura should create a **Health Journey**.

A journey should contain:

* When it started
* Condition / issue name
* Doctor
* Hospital / clinic
* Current medications
* Tests and reports
* Important observations
* Previous consultations
* Timeline
* Follow-ups
* Uploaded files

The user should be able to continuously add updates to that journey.

---

# 3. Home Screen

Keep the home screen extremely simple.

### Header

**Good morning, Sharon 👋**

**Nura**
*Your family's health, remembered.*

Do NOT show:

* “Personal Vault”
* “Private Space”
* “Find People”
* Social features
* Community features
* Complex analytics
* Unnecessary health scores

---

## Main Home Screen Sections

### Family

Show family members as simple profile cards.

Example:

**Sharon**
You

**Mom**
2 ongoing health journeys

**Dad**
1 ongoing health journey

**Riya**
Child

Allow the user to add up to **5–10 family members**.

Primary CTA:

### `+ Add Family Member`

That's it.

Do not add a “Find People” feature.

---

# 4. Add Family Member Flow

Make this extremely simple.

### Screen: Add Family Member

Title:

**Who would you like to add?**

Options:

* Myself
* Mother
* Father
* Spouse
* Child
* Sibling
* Other

Then collect only essential information:

* Name
* Date of birth / age
* Relationship
* Gender
* Optional profile photo

CTA:

**Add Member**

Do not ask for a huge amount of information during onboarding.

The user can add health information later.
