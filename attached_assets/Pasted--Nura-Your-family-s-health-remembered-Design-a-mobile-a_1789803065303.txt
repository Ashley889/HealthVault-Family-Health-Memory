# Nura

### Your family's health, remembered.

Design a **mobile application**, NOT a web application or desktop dashboard.

Nura is a simple, calm, family-focused health memory app that helps people keep track of their family's medical history, health issues, reports, medications, doctors and important health events in one place.

The experience should feel like a **beautiful, modern mobile consumer app**, not hospital software.

---

## 1. Design Direction

Use a **soft sky-blue + white theme**.

### Visual style

* Light sky blue
* White
* Very subtle blue-grey
* Dark blue-grey for text
* Soft rounded cards
* Large readable typography
* Minimal icons
* Generous whitespace
* Clean iOS/Android-style mobile UI
* Subtle borders
* Very light shadows

Avoid:

* Dark themes
* Purple
* Heavy gradients
* Bright green medical UI
* Red-heavy medical alerts
* Complicated dashboards
* Excessive charts
* Dense tables

The app should feel:

**Calm • Personal • Trustworthy • Simple • Premium**

# 6. Important: Short-Term vs Long-Term Health Issues

When the user adds a new health issue, ask:

### “How long do you expect this to need attention?”

Two choices:

### Short-term

**Something temporary that may resolve in weeks or months.**

Examples:

* Fever
* Shoulder pain
* Minor injury
* Infection

### Long-term

**Something that may need ongoing monitoring or management.**

Examples:

* Thyroid
* PCOS / PCOD
* Diabetes
* Hypertension
* Asthma
* Pregnancy
* Other ongoing conditions

This choice determines how Nura organizes the information.

# 15. AI — Keep It Simple

Do not make AI the main screen.

AI should work quietly in the background.

Use AI for:

### Organizing

Convert:

> “Went to doctor because I've been feeling tired. Doctor asked me to do thyroid test.”

into:

**Doctor Visit**
Reason: Fatigue
Recommended test: Thyroid profile

### Summarizing

Create a simple summary of a health journey.

### Connecting

Automatically connect a new report to the appropriate health journey.

### Reminders

Identify follow-ups mentioned by the user.

### Missing information

Example:

> “You added a prescription but haven't added the prescribing doctor.”

Do NOT diagnose conditions.

Do NOT recommend changing medication.

Do NOT generate medical treatment plans.

# 18. Final Product Feeling

The app should communicate one simple idea:

> **“I don't have to remember everything about my family's health. Nura remembers it for me.”**

### Nura

**Your family's health, remembered.**

Build this as a **mobile-first consumer app**, with realistic family and medical information based on the uploaded report, rather than a generic web dashboard.
